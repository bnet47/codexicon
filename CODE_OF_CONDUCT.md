# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Codexicon is intended to be a practical, respectful place to improve public development tooling. Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and accessibility needs;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, stalking, doxxing, and sustained disruption;
- protect private information, credentials, vulnerability details, and confidential material;
- accept a maintainer's reasonable moderation decision even when disagreeing with it.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, security reports, and other spaces where someone is representing Codexicon.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's **Report a vulnerability** flow and begin the report title with `Conduct report`. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue. If that flow is unavailable, contact [@bnet47](https://github.com/bnet47) through a private contact method published on the profile.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as reasonably possible. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is prohibited. The maintainer will consider context, impact, history, and safety when deciding a response.
